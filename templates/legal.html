Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

SaliBot is a personal portfolio project by Salima Iddrisu, built to learn and demonstrate agentic AI. It is free, has no ads, and does not sell or share your data for marketing.

What SaliBot collects

  • Your account: your name and email address. If you use "Continue with Google", Google shares only your name, email address and profile picture link with SaliBot; SaliBot never sees your Google password.
  • Your password, if you create an email account. It is stored only as a salted, one-way hash, never as plain text.
  • Your chats: the messages you send and SaliBot's replies, chat titles and pins.
  • Things you ask SaliBot to remember, until you ask it to forget them or delete the chat.
  • Files you upload (documents and pictures) and voice recordings you send for transcription.

How it is used

Only to run SaliBot for you: to answer your questions, keep your chat history, and let you log in. Your chats are private to your account; other users cannot see them.

Services that process your data

To answer you, SaliBot sends the relevant text, files or audio to these services:

  • Google Gemini (Google LLC): writes the answers, reads pictures and documents, transcribes voice and watches YouTube links you share.
  • Tavily: runs web searches when SaliBot needs current information.
  • arXiv: searched when you ask for research papers.
  • LangSmith (LangChain): records how SaliBot handled each request, including the messages, so problems can be found and fixed.
  • Neon: the database where accounts, chats and memories are stored.
  • Render: the server that runs SaliBot.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any chat from its menu in the sidebar. This removes its messages, memories, uploaded files and document search data.
  • Ask SaliBot to forget something you told it.
  • To delete your whole account, open an issue on SaliBot's GitHub page and it will be removed.

Please don't share sensitive information

SaliBot is a learning project, not a professional service. Please don't upload or type passwords, bank details, health records or other sensitive personal information.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
